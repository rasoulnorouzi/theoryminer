## The papers that you download

`download_papers()` downloads open-access copies, and it obeys `robots.txt` by default. The
licence of each paper is in the `licence` column of its result. A licence such as CC BY permits
you to share the text; other licences do not. Check the licence before you publish the text of a
paper, for example in a notebook output.
